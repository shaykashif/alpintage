How it works

01 · Classify

How two markets relate

Pternas cross-compares thousands of cultural and economic events on Kalshi and Polymarket. For each pair, a frontier classification model reads both markets' full resolution rules and returns a probability for each relationship: A implies B, B implies A, mutually exclusive, or exhaustive.

02 · Confirm

Probabilities, not prose

Because the output is a calculated probability rather than an LLM's free text, each judgment can be calibrated with thresholds, tuned against hand-labeled pairs and required to hold in both orders, which can make it reliable enough to act on without human review.

03 · Trade on paper

Re-priced every three seconds

Once a relationship is confirmed, Pternas re-prices the pair every three seconds and paper-trades any price that violates it, buying every leg of the position or none. No real orders are ever placed.

Why a classifier

Logic, not matching titles

Most cross-market arbitrage models match similar market titles deterministically. Pternas judges how markets are logically related instead, in about 0.3 seconds per pair, run in parallel across many pairs, so it can screen thousands of pairs a rules-based matcher would never connect. An LLM can take several seconds per judgment.
